## EPCForge v1.2.1 Release Notes

### Added
- BOQ progress billing: interactive milestone selection dialog when creating a Sales Invoice from a BOQ, displaying a real-time summary card of gross amount, advance deduction, retention deduction, target date, and estimated net payable.
- Automatic Budget Cost Head resolution: Purchase Orders and Purchase Invoices automatically infer their `epc_cost_head` from linked BOQ items (`item_type` mapping), linked Purchase Orders, subcontracting status, or item characteristics.

### Fixed
- Multi-currency support on Project Budget: committed and actual cost calculations now roll up company base currency (`base_grand_total` and `base_costing_amount`), properly applying transaction exchange rates to foreign-currency Purchase Orders, Invoices, and Timesheets.
- BOQ item actual cost calculation in `boq.py` now aggregates `base_amount` from Purchase Receipt and Purchase Invoice items instead of transaction currency amounts.
- Project Dashboard procurement coverage now aggregates `base_amount` from Purchase Order and Purchase Receipt items.
- Project Budget line item fallback: submitted Purchase Orders and Invoices without an explicit header cost head are attributed to their corresponding budget head using item BOQ links.
- Purchase Order form layout: moved `epc_cost_head` into the Accounting Dimensions section (after `cost_center`) so it is clearly visible rather than buried in the collapsed Freight section.
